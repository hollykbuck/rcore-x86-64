pub(crate) fn device_init()

Initialize the platform devices and their interrupt routing (ch9).

x86-64 has no PLIC: device interrupts reach the local APIC through the IO-APIC (for the legacy 16550 UART, ISA IRQ4 = IO-APIC pin 4) or MSI-X (for the PCI virtio devices). Each routed vector gets a handler registered in crate::trap::register_irq.
